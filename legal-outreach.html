The 4-Stage Sequence

Every message below is sent automatically on your firm's behalf when a case balance goes past due — with the case number in every subject line and body. Highlighted tokens are filled per case automatically. Nothing activates until you approve this sequence in writing.

⚖️ Compliance gates active: No legal threats appear in any automated message — demand language is attorney-drafted only. No medical details beyond case number and matter type are ever included (PHI minimization). A dispute filed at any stage pauses all outreach instantly and routes to your queue with the full record.

Attorney Escalation — Nothing Sends
Day 40 past due · Internal flag only
Flag Only
Automated outreach ends here — permanently. At day 40, the case moves to your Attorney Review queue with the complete file attached:
  • Every notice sent, with timestamps and delivery status
  • All portal activity — opens, clicks, payment attempts, messages
  • Any captured settlement offers from the negotiation assistant

Any demand letter, lien action, or litigation step is drafted and sent by you directly — never automated.

Standing Rules (Always Active)

⏸️Dispute = instant pause. The moment a responsible party files a question or dispute at any stage, all outreach on that case stops and the matter routes to your queue with a 48-business-hour review target.
🛡️Bounded settlement authority. The portal and negotiation assistant can accept resolutions only within the discount ceiling you approve per case at intake (e.g., 10%). Offers below your floor are captured and routed — never accepted or declined by AI.
📋Complete audit trail. Every message, response, and portal event is timestamped to the case file — dispute-ready documentation, automatically.
🔒PHI minimization. Case number and matter type only. No diagnosis, treatment, or patient detail ever appears in outreach.
